Dosa

Dosa is a quintessential South Indian staple that has captured the hearts and palates of food lovers worldwide. At its core, this iconic dish is a thin, savory crepe made from a fermented batter of rice and black lentils (urad dal), which gives it a distinctively tangy flavor profile and an airy, light texture. Preparing the perfect dosa is an art form: the batter is ladled onto a scorching-hot flat griddle (tawa) and spread swiftly in concentric circles using the back of a ladle. As it cooks, a generous drizzle of ghee or oil helps it achieve a beautifully golden, shatteringly crisp exterior while maintaining a soft, pliable interior. Traditionally served piping hot, a classic dosa is rarely eaten alone; it is almost always accompanied by a bowl of flavorful sambar (a vegetable and lentil stew) and a variety of chutneys, most notably coconut, tomato, and mint.
